# Prototype Brief

## Audience and framing

The prototype does not claim to represent all autistic people, and it does not claim to diagnose or treat autism.

It explores a specific experience profile associated in research with cognitive flexibility and with first-person reports of difficulty starting, stopping, and changing activities.

## First playable question

**Was the stabilizer coupler actually replaced?**

The player finds a maintenance record that says the coupler was replaced. The investigation is interrupted. On return, the player compares the record with the physical replacement coupler and serial information in Storage.

## Core lesson

The mechanic should help the player notice the cost of losing context and experiment with an external context-preservation strategy.

Quick Pin is not presented as a cure, score, or mandatory correct behavior. It is an available strategy.

## Runtime target

Target 5–10 minutes for the class vertical slice. The larger 25–30 minute mystery remains post-prototype scope.
