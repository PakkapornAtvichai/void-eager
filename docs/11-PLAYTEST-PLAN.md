# Playtest Plan

## What we are testing
Not clinical efficacy. We are testing whether the prototype communicates and supports the intended gameplay loop.

## Minimum sample
At least 3 fresh players if possible. One can be a teammate who did not implement the current screen.

## Observe without coaching
- Does the player understand what they are investigating?
- Do they notice the interruption?
- Do they understand Quick Pin before choosing?
- After the secondary task, can they say what they were doing before?
- Do they understand how to resume?
- Can they connect the replacement record to the physical coupler evidence?
- Can they make the deduction without being told the answer?
- Does the reflection wording feel judgmental or neutral?

## Record
- completion time
- where help was requested
- whether Quick Pin was used
- whether the player remembered the prior thread
- confusion points
- bugs/softlocks
- one sentence: "What did you think the game was about?"

## Success threshold
The loop is considered demo-ready when a fresh player can finish it without developer explanation and can describe interruption/context recovery as part of the experience.

## Do not infer
Do not infer autism traits, diagnosis, impairment, or treatment effect from a class playtest.
